Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What We Collect

When you submit a quote request, we collect the information you provide: name, email address, property details, timeline, and any photos or links you share. We do not collect payment card information directly — that is handled entirely by Stripe.

How We Use It

  • To prepare and send you a project quote
  • To communicate with you about your project
  • To produce your finished video
  • To send a payment link once a quote is approved

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Photos You Submit

Photos submitted for a project are used only to produce your video, unless you separately give permission for portfolio use.

Third-Party Services

Quote requests are processed through FormSubmit; payments are processed through Stripe. Each service has its own privacy practices governing the data they handle on our behalf.

Data Retention

We retain project information and communications for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill your order and for basic business record-keeping.

Contact

Questions about this policy or requests to delete your information can be sent through our contact form.
