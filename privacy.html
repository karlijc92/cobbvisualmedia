Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Cobb Visual Media ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through cobbvisualmedia.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to send us through our quote request form, including:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your business or property name
  • Project details such as service type, location, timeline, project size, budget range, and notes
  • Links to photos, listings, or other materials you share

When you pay for a project, payment details are collected directly by our payment processor. We never see or store your full card number.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your request and prepare your quote
  • Communicate with you about your project, scheduling, and delivery
  • Send payment links and keep records of completed work

We don't sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we don't add you to marketing lists without your permission.

3. Services we rely on

We use a few trusted third-party services to run this website and our business. They handle your information only as needed to provide their service:

  • FormSubmit processes quote form submissions and delivers them to our inbox, along with an automatic confirmation email to you.
  • Stripe processes payments securely.
  • Zoho Mail hosts our business email.
  • Vercel hosts this website and may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and browser type, for security and performance.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts on this site, which means your browser connects to Google's servers when pages load.

4. Cookies and tracking

We don't use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or tracking pixels on this site. If that changes, we'll update this policy first.

5. Your photos and project materials

Photos, footage, and other materials you share are used only to create your project. With your permission, finished work may appear in our portfolio, as described in our Terms of Service. If you'd prefer your project not be featured, just let us know.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep project and contact information for as long as needed to complete your project, maintain business and tax records, and support any future work you request. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, except where we're required to keep it for legal or accounting reasons.

7. Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we have about you. Reach out through our contact form, and we'll respond promptly.

8. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for adults and businesses. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with its effective date.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out through our contact form and we'll get back to you.
