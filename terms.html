Terms of Service

Last updated: September 2026

Services

Cobb Visual Media creates cinematic walkthrough videos for properties and businesses, built from photos provided by the client or captured by us upon request.

Quotes & Pricing

Pricing is determined per project based on property size, photo/shoot requirements, revisions needed, and turnaround time. No work begins until a quote is approved in writing (email) by the client.

Payment

Full payment is due upfront via the secure payment link provided after quote approval. Work begins once payment is confirmed. We do not store or process card details directly — all payments are handled through Stripe.

Revisions

Each project includes one round of revisions to the delivered video. Additional revision rounds may incur an added fee, communicated before any extra work begins.

Turnaround

Standard turnaround time will be communicated at the time of quote approval and depends on current workload. Rush delivery may be available for an additional fee.

Cancellations & Refunds

Because production begins immediately upon payment, completed or in-progress work is non-refundable. If a project has not yet entered production, cancellation requests will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

Photo & Content Usage

By submitting photos, you confirm you have the right to use them and grant Cobb Visual Media permission to use those photos solely for the purpose of producing your video. Cobb Visual Media may request permission to display the finished video in its own portfolio; this is never assumed without your consent.

Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent through our contact form.
